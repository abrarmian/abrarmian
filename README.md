# Abrar Hussain Mian

**Doctoral Researcher | University of Oulu, Finland**

I am a doctoral researcher working at the intersection of **plant–microbe interactions, phytoremediation, fungal biology and bioinformatics**. My research focuses on understanding how plant-associated fungi influence metal uptake, accumulation and tolerance, with particular interest in environmentally sustainable remediation under cold-climate conditions.

My broader interests include fungal adaptation to metal stress, aquatic moss-based remediation, environmental microbiology and the application of genomic and bioinformatic approaches to understand plant–fungus–environment interactions.

## Current Research

My doctoral research investigates the role of the aquatic moss *Warnstorfia fluitans* and associated fungal endophytes in metal remediation under cold-climate conditions.

My current research activities include:

- Evaluating metal removal and accumulation in aquatic moss systems
- Investigating fungal contributions to metal tolerance and metal fate
- Characterizing fungal isolates using morphological and molecular approaches
- Developing fungal genomics and comparative genomics workflows
- Curating ITS and LSU reference sequences for fungal identification
- Performing multiple sequence alignment and maximum-likelihood phylogenetic analysis
- Developing reproducible workflows for scientific data analysis and visualization

## International Research Experience

### NEPHEWS Early-Stage Researcher Programme – TARLA, Türkiye

In 2026, I completed a research internship at the **Turkish Accelerator and Radiation Laboratory (TARLA)** in Ankara through the **NEPHEWS Early-Stage Researcher Programme**.

The internship provided hands-on experience with accelerator-based research infrastructure and nuclear analytical methods, including:

- TARLA accelerator infrastructure and operational principles
- Beamline and pneumatic sample irradiation systems
- HPGe and Clover gamma spectroscopy detector systems
- Sample preparation for nuclear activation analysis
- Gamma-spectrum evaluation and elemental analysis
- Energy and efficiency calibration
- Dead-time correction
- Analysis of Cu and Ni activation products

The visit strengthened my experience in applying advanced analytical infrastructure to environmental and biological research.
